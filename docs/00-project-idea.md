# Project Idea

Daily 3 is a minimal iOS productivity app built around one simple idea:
choose only three important things to focus on each day.

Traditional to-do lists can easily become endless collections of tasks.
Daily 3 takes a different approach. Instead of asking “What do I need to do?”,
it asks:

> What are the three things that matter most today?

## The Idea

Each day, the user chooses up to three priorities.

Tasks can be completed throughout the day, with progress shown simply as
0/3, 1/3, 2/3, or 3/3.

Unfinished tasks do not automatically carry over to the next day.
Every new day is a fresh decision about what deserves attention.

## Looking Back

A calendar keeps a visual history of daily progress.

Days are represented with different activity levels based on how many
priorities were completed:

- 0/3 — no completed priorities
- 1/3 — light activity
- 2/3 — medium activity
- 3/3 — full completion

The visualization is inspired by GitHub's contribution graph, making
progress and consistency easy to understand at a glance.

Users can select a previous day to see what their priorities were.

## Beyond Today

Daily 3 will also provide simple statistics such as completion rate,
completed tasks, and 3/3 days.

Notifications, a home screen widget, and an Apple Watch companion can
make the daily three priorities easier to keep visible throughout the day.

## Product Principle

Daily 3 should remain simple as it grows.

New features should support the core idea rather than turn the app into
another complex task manager.

**Three priorities. One day. Clear progress.**
